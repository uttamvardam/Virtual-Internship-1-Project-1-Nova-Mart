### Q8. Which product recorded the highest Incremental Revenue Percentage (IR%) during the Sankranti campaign? What was its IR%?

**Business Objective:**

Identify the product that achieved the highest percentage growth in revenue after the Sankranti promotion.

**Analysis Approach:**

- Filter the Sankranti campaign.
- Calculate revenue before and after promotions using price multiplied by quantity sold.
- Aggregate revenue by product.
- Calculate Incremental Revenue Percentage (IR%).
- Identify the product with the highest IR%.
- Match the product code with its product name.

**IR% Formula:**

IR% = ((Revenue After - Revenue Before) / Revenue Before) × 100

**Answer:**

| Product | IR% |
|---|---:|
| Atliq_Suflower_Oil (1L) | 91.83% |

**Highest IR%: 91.83%**

**Business Insight:**

Atliq_Suflower_Oil (1L) recorded the highest incremental revenue percentage during Sankranti, with revenue increasing by approximately 91.83% compared with its pre-promotion baseline.
